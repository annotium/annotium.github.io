Annotium has two home workspaces. Use the grid button in the home app bar to switch between them.

## Projects

Use **Projects** when you want to reopen an annotation and continue editing its objects. Projects can be organized in folders and can include project notes.

The completed article will cover:

- Creating work from Gallery, Camera, or Compose.
- Preserving editable annotation objects.
- Organizing projects with folders, rename, move, duplicate, and delete.
- The free-tier flattened-copy behavior when reopening saved work.

## Photos

Use **Photos** to browse albums and annotate images from the device photo
library. Edits from this workspace are written back as images and do not create items in the Projects grid.

## Switch workspaces

1. On either home screen, tap the grid button in the top-left corner.
2. Choose **Projects** or **Photos**.
3. Confirm that the title and grid content change to the selected workspace.
