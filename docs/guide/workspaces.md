# Projects and Photos

Tap the workspace switcher at the top left of the home screen to choose **Projects** or **Photos**. The heading and grid change when you switch.

<img src="/images/getting-started/switching.jpg" alt="The workspace switcher shows Projects and Photos." width="360" />

| Workspace | Choose it when | Saved result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Projects** | You want folders, project notes, or the ability to revisit individual annotations. | An editable project. Reopening its existing annotations requires Premium. |
| **Photos** | You want to mark up a photo from a device album. | A flattened image with the annotations baked in. The default destination is the device photo library. |
