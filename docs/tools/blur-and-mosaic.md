# Blur and Mosaic

Use **Blur** to soften an area or **Mosaic** to replace it with blocks.

<img src="/images/tools/blur/blur-result.webp" alt="Annotium's Blur tool softens handwritten details in a selected area of a sample invoice." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

## Mosaic

<img src="/images/tools/mosaic/mosaic-result.webp" alt="A rectangular mosaic effect covers part of a notice on a garage door." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

1. Choose **Blur** or **Mosaic** from the privacy tools.
2. Drag over the detail you want to hide.
3. Resize the area and inspect the full-size result before sharing.

The Blur example uses a CC0 sample invoice with personal details removed. The Mosaic example uses a public garage photo. For private material, verify that the detail cannot be read in the exported copy.
