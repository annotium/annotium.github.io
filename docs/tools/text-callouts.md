  Outline · This page is ready for verified steps and current screenshots.

## Add text

- Draw a text area and enter its content.
- Reopen an existing label for editing.
- Resize the text container without losing legibility.

## Text containers

- Plain text.
- Rounded rectangle text.
- Oval text.
- [Rounded pointer callout](/tools/callout-rectangle) (Callout (Rectangle)).
- Oval pointer callout.

### Callout (Oval)

Use an oval callout for a short note when the pointer should identify a nearby detail.

<img src="/images/tools/callout-oval-result.webp" alt="An oval callout reading Check clearance points to a hinge on a weathered wooden door." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Choose **Callout (Oval)**, enter a concise label, then position the pointer tip on the detail. This manual iPhone 18 capture shows the callout over a hinge.

## Expressive callouts

- Cloud text (Premium).
- Burst text (Premium).
- Thought bubble text (Premium).

<img src="/images/tools/cloud-text-result.webp" alt="A red outlined Cloud Text bubble reading Review sits over a blank clipboard on a straw background." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Cloud Text can place a short thought or status label in a cloud-shaped bubble.

<img src="/images/tools/burst-text-result.webp" alt="A red outlined Burst Text shape reading Check points toward a crack in a rough stone surface." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Use Burst Text to make a brief inspection note stand out next to the defect.

<img src="/images/tools/thought-bubble-result.webp" alt="A red outlined Thought Bubble reading Alternative floats above the blank paper on a clipboard." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Thought Bubble works for a short idea or alternative on a planning surface.

## Format text

- Choose a font family and size.
- Change text color and opacity.
- Apply bold, italic, strikethrough, and alignment.
- Style the callout outline and fill independently where supported.
