# Loupe

Show a close-up of a small detail while keeping its location visible in the original photo.

<img src="/images/tool-scenes/cracked-concrete-wall.jpg" alt="A close-up photograph of a crack across a concrete wall." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

## Highlight a small detail

1. Choose **Loupe** from the focus tools.
2. Place the lens over open space near the detail.
3. Move the source pointer to the area you want to magnify.
4. Adjust the lens size and magnification until the detail is clear.

The Loupe is an annotation on the image. It magnifies a selected detail; it does not zoom or move the canvas.

::: tip Projects or Photos?
Use **Projects** to keep the Loupe editable. Use **Photos** to work from your device library and save a finished image there.
:::
