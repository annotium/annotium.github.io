# Loupe

Show a close-up without hiding where it came from.

<img src="/images/tools/loupe/loupe-result.webp" alt="A circular loupe magnifies a plumbing connection while its pointer marks the source in a sink repair photo." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

1. Choose **Loupe** from the focus tools.
2. Draw to place the lens.
3. Move the source point to the detail and adjust the lens.

The Loupe magnifies part of the image. It does not zoom or move the canvas.
