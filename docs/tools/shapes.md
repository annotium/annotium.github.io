## Basic shapes

- Rectangle, including corner radius.
- Oval.
- Triangle.
- Polygon with additional points.

## Advanced shapes

- Parallelogram (Premium).
- Arc, including open and closed states (Premium).
- Donut or ring (Premium).
- Coordinate shape (Premium).

## Style a shape

- Turn the stroke and fill on or off where supported.
- Set outline width, color, and opacity.
- Choose a solid or patterned fill.
- Resize and reshape with handles.

## Suggested examples

Use one real task per tool family: frame a defect, circle a control, outline an
irregular area, and mark an annular part.
