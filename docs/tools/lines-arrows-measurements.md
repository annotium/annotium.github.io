## Point out a feature

- [Arrow](/tools/arrow) points from open space to a specific detail.
- A straight **Line** marks an edge or connects two points.
- **Curve** follows a curved path.
- **Connector** links objects or locations in a diagram.

## Measurement tools

- [**Line Measure**](/tools/line-measure) (Premium) labels a span directly across its endpoints.
- **Angle Measure** marks the angle between two arms.
- **Leader Line** (Premium) routes a pointer from a detail to a label area.
- [Dimension Line](/tools/dimension-line) (Premium) marks a distance between two points with a dimension rail.
- **Tick Measure** (Premium) marks a line with repeated tick marks.
- **Coordinate** (Premium) marks a point against coordinate axes.

**Radius** and **Diameter** are in the Shapes group. Both measurement tools require Premium.

## Shared properties

- Stroke color, width, opacity, and dash style.
- Head and tail decoration.
- Label position for supported measurement tools.
- Orientation and coordinate display for supported tools.

## Draw accurately

- Use **Snap** for common angles.
- Zoom before placing endpoints.
- Adjust individual handles after drawing.
- Explain how to add points to multi-segment tools where supported.

::: warning Photo measurements
Values depend on point placement, scale, and image perspective. Do not present
them as certified measurements without a calibrated workflow.
:::
