## Magnify a detail

- Add a [Loupe](/tools/loupe) object.
- Move the source area and magnified lens.
- Adjust the magnification ratio.

## Spotlight an area

- Draw a freeform spotlight.
- Draw a rectangular spotlight.
- Choose the spotlight style where supported.

<img src="/images/tools/spotlight-result.webp" alt="A circular Spotlight isolates the wiring inside an exposed wall socket while the surrounding photo area is darkened; selection handles are visible." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Use a circular Spotlight to focus attention on a small detail, such as the wiring in this exposed socket.

## Obscure private information

- Use [Blur or Mosaic](/tools/blur-and-mosaic) to cover a selected area.
- Adjust pixel cell size and effect amount.

## Advanced pixel effects
