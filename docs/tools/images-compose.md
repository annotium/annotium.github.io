## Insert an image

- Explain the Premium requirement before opening the picker.
- Pick an image and place it over the base image.
- Move and resize the inserted image.

<img src="/images/tools/insert-image-result.webp" alt="A small hinge photo is inserted at the upper left of a garage-door photo and selected with its resize outline visible." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Keep the inserted photo small enough to leave the base image visible. Select it to show the outline and positioning controls.

## Copy an image region

- Select the copy tool and mark a region.
- Reposition the copied region.
- Confirm or cancel the copy operation.

<img src="/images/tools/copy-region-result.webp" alt="A rectangular copy of the exposed masonry and plaster is placed below its source area on the wall photo; selection handles show the copy can be moved." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Copy Region creates a movable duplicate of the selected photo area. The example keeps the original wall visible above the selected copy.

## Crop

- Enter crop mode.
- Move each edge or corner.
- Confirm or cancel the crop.
- Explain that cropping changes the project's base image.

<img src="/images/tools/crop-frame.webp" alt="The Crop preview frame surrounds the garage door and both notices, with a grid and corner handles visible." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Adjust the frame to keep the door and its notices, then tap **Crop** to apply it. This screen shows the preview before confirmation.

<img src="/images/tools/crop-result.webp" alt="The cropped garage photo fills the canvas, keeping the complete door and both notices while removing most of the outer wall and pavement." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

After confirmation, the crop becomes the project's base photo.

## Lasso

<img src="/images/tools/lasso-selection.webp" alt="A yellow dashed Lasso selection surrounds the damaged plaster opening and some nearby wall; Crop and Erase actions are available below." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Trace around the region to select. This preview shows the irregular selection before choosing **Crop** to keep it or **Erase** to remove it.

<img src="/images/tools/lasso-crop-result.webp" alt="The Lasso crop keeps the damaged plaster opening as an irregular cutout, with white canvas around the selected region." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

Choosing **Crop** keeps the lassoed area and removes the surrounding photo. Use **Erase** to remove the selected area instead.

<img src="/images/tools/lasso-erase-result.webp" alt="The lassoed damaged opening has been erased, leaving a large white area in the plaster wall while the surrounding wall remains." style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;" />

The Erase result removes the selected area and leaves a blank region in its place.

## Compose photos

Choose a composition mode, then review the arrangement before you finish.

<img src="/images/tools/images-compose/compose-grid-result.webp" alt="The Compose screen previews four selected construction and electrical photos in a 4-grid collage, with 4-row and 4-big-top layouts, spacing controls, and source thumbnails." />

This example shows four selected photos in the **4-grid** layout. The other visible layout choices are **4-row** and **4-big-top**; the thumbnails below the controls show the selected source photos.

<img src="/images/tools/images-compose/stitch-vertical-result.webp" alt="The Compose screen previews two plumbing-construction photos joined vertically, with the vertical stitch mode selected and both source thumbnails visible." />

Choose **Stitch vertically** to join photos from top to bottom. Here, two plumbing-construction photos form one continuous view, and their thumbnails remain available for reordering or removal.

Composition modes include:

- Collage.
- Stitch vertically.
- Stitch horizontally.
- Add, reorder, and remove source photos.
